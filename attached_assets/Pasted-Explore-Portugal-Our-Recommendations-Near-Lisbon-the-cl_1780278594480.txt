Explore Portugal — Our Recommendations

Near Lisbon — the closest airport to Évora (1.5h by car or train)
Visit the towns of Cascais and Sintra, and explore Lisbon's neighborhoods of Campo de Ourique, Mouraria, and Alfama.
For beaches, we recommend driving south to any of the following: Costa da Caparica, Arrábida Natural Park, or Praia da Comporta. 
Venture North — Porto (4h from Évora and 3h from Lisbon)
Visit the city of Porto, stroll along the Ribeira waterfront, and explore the famous wine cellars across the river in Vila Nova de Gaia.
For wine lovers, spend a day in the stunning Douro Valley, one of the world's oldest wine regions. Nearby, we also recommend visiting the charming towns of Braga and Guimarães 
Venture South — Algarve Region (3h from Évora and Lisbon)
The Algarve is known for its golden cliffs, hidden coves, and some of Europe's best beaches. We recommend staying near Lagos, Tavira, or Albufeira depending on your preferred pace. For beaches, it's hard to go wrong—our favorites include Praia da Arrifana, visit the Sagres cape, and the long sandy stretches around Ilha de Tavira.

For the true adventurers: You can drive to Sevilla, Spain (3h from Évora), discover the southern coast of Spain - Marbella, Malaga…  (4-5h from Évora) OR take a 1.5h flight to Morocco - we’ve done all that! 




Travel & Stay — Évora

Recommended stay: 2 nights (Friday and Saturday). Évora can be explored in a day or two and is best enjoyed as part of a longer trip through Portugal.
About Évora
Évora is a UNESCO World Heritage city in the heart of the Alentejo region, known for its Roman ruins, medieval streets, and excellent food and wine.
Getting There
Évora is 1.5 hours from Lisbon by car, bus, or train. We recommend flying into Lisbon Airport (LIS) and either renting a car or taking the direct train.
Where to Stay
We recommend staying within the city walls for Friday and Saturday. Boutique hotels and guesthouses fill up quickly, so we encourage booking early.
What to Do
Visit the Roman Temple, Chapel of Bones, and Cathedral, wander the historic streets, and enjoy Alentejo cuisine and wines. The city is compact, walkable, and easy to experience in just a few hours.
